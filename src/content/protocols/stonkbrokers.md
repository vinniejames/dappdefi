StonkBrokers is an Anvil NFTFi market on Robinhood Chain: a fixed-supply 4,444 ERC-721 collection with an NFT AMM and NFT-backed loans, where ETH trade and borrow fees fund tokenized stock dividends for activated brokers.

Users buy and sell StonkBroker NFTs against the `$STONKBROKER` ERC-20 in the AMM vault, or borrow against NFT collateral. Every fill and loan charges an ETH fee split on-chain — 70% to StockBooster, which buys tokenized equities (AAPL, AMZN, NVDA) and airdrops them to activated broker TBAs, and 30% to the protocol fee sink.

Spot Uniswap V4 ETH/STONKBROKER liquidity is separate from the NFTFi market. The product sits at the intersection of NFT finance and Robinhood Chain's tokenized stock rails.
